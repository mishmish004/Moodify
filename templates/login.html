Playlists for the mood you're in

Tell Moodify how you feel or what you're doing, in any words. It plans the parts, picks songs that fit your taste, and saves a private playlist to your Spotify.

{# POST, not a link: starting the Spotify login from a GET lets prefetchers and crawlers use up the one-time state #}

Friends only. Your Spotify account has to be added first; ask whoever sent you the link.

What it looks like

You write

"chill summer morning, then 30 min workout, then instantly lock in for coding"

Moodify plans

  1. Summer morningeasy start · energy 4
  2. Workout30 min · energy 8
  3. Lock in, straight insteady · energy 5

Then it finds each song on Spotify and saves the playlist to your account.

What happens to your data

What goes to OpenAI
Each mood you type, with your answers to the taste questions and a summary of your Spotify listening, so its model can suggest songs. The summary covers your most played artists and tracks, what you played recently, some of your liked songs, the artists you follow and your playlists' names. Up to 150 songs you know are listed too, so some can go into your playlists. Not your email or your Spotify login.
What Moodify keeps
Your Spotify ID and name, a login token and which Spotify permissions you gave, your answers, that summary and song list (refreshed daily), and the playlists it made for you with their tracks.
What it can do in your Spotify
Read your listening history, liked songs, followed artists and playlists; create playlists in your account; add songs to your liked songs when you like them in Moodify; and, when you press Play now, add songs to your queue.
How to delete it
"Disconnect and delete my data", at the bottom of the home page, deletes everything Moodify stored about you, straight away. Playlists already in your Spotify stay there. To remove its access to Spotify as well, go to spotify.com/account/apps.
