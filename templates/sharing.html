Sharing with friends

Everyone on this Moodify is a friend of whoever runs it (at most 5 people). Nothing about you reaches them unless you switch it on here.

Whatever you switch on, these are never shown to or used for anyone else: what you type for a playlist, your reviews and ratings, what Moodify learned from them, playlists made when you were going through something hard, and your {{ svc.name }} login. Moodify never uses your {{ svc.name }} login for a friend's playlist.

Switching something off works at once. Playlists friends already made stay in their accounts; they're theirs.
