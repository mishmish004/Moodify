Sharing with friends

Everyone on this Moodify is a friend of whoever runs it (at most 5 people). Nothing about you reaches them unless you switch it on here.

Friends can see your taste profile (the summary Moodify made from your Spotify listening and your answers), your top artists, your place on the taste map, and your activity: the titles and dates of playlists Moodify made for you. Never what you typed. A friend can make one playlist from a prompt and several people's taste, yours included. Moodify uses your stored taste and music library for it, and saves the playlist in their Spotify, not yours. A friend can make a playlist "the way you'd hear it", from your stored taste and music library. It's saved in their Spotify. Nothing is ever added to yours.

Whatever you switch on, these are never shown to or used for anyone else: what you type for a playlist, your reviews and ratings, what Moodify learned from them, playlists made when you were going through something hard, and your Spotify login. Moodify never uses your Spotify login for a friend's playlist.

Switching something off works at once. Playlists friends already made stay in their Spotify; they're theirs.
